running `migrations/0001_core.sql` and `0002_functions_rls.sql`, then its

## The three 0.1 drifts, now resolved in the baseline

- `staff_roles` RLS — **ON** in the rebuild ✅
- per-line commission `supplier_net` formula — present (column exists, generated) ✅
- invoice/line columns from the Randox import (commission_pct, line_type,
  supplier_contact, client_id, outstanding, vat, xero_id) — all present ✅

## The only differences, and why they don't matter

The auto-generated NOT NULL constraint *names* differ between the two databases
(`2200_17548_…` vs `2200_17551_…`). These are internal, OID-based identifiers
Postgres assigns per database — the constraints themselves are identical, only
their internal serial numbers differ. This is expected for any two separately
built databases and is not a structural difference.

## One thing not directly visible in the export

The export lists that `supplier_net` exists and is generated, but not its
generation *formula*. The baseline defines it as
`round(supplier_gross * (1 - commission_pct/100), 2)`, matching the production
behaviour verified during the Randox work. Worth a functional check in 0.4 when
seed data exists (insert a line, confirm the net computes correctly).

**The repository can recreate the production database structure from scratch.**
