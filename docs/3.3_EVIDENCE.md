### Follow-ups raised by the live data — RESOLVED (11 Aug 2026)
**Test records removed from production.** Investigation found the artefacts of
today's 1.5 audit-log testing: client "Audit Test" (0 campaigns) and **two
duplicate leads both named "Test"** (0 tasks). Verified nothing was attached
(0 campaigns / contacts / invoices), then deleted the source records and their
organisation rows. Result: 1 client (Randox Health, 21 campaigns), 0 leads,
7 organisations (Randox + 6 suppliers). Deletions are captured in the 1.5 audit log.

Note: the duplicate "Test" leads are a live demonstration of the problem the new
model solves — the migration collapsed them to a single organisation, and the
`organisations_name_key` unique index prevents the duplicate recurring.

**Vendor count explained:** 3.1 counted 7 distinct vendor strings but 6 supplier
organisations were created — consistent with a blank or case/whitespace-duplicate
vendor being correctly skipped. No action needed.
